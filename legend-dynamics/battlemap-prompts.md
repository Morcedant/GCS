# Battle map prompts for the playtest adventures

Prompts for an image tool (ChatGPT, Midjourney, Gemini, or similar) to make a top-down battle map for each scene of the four playtest adventures in the GM's Guides. Each map has no grid: the GM view's battle map draws its own hexes over the picture. Set the hex size the scene lists.

For a whole adventure at once, paste its **series prompt** into a chat-based image tool and say "next" after each map. For one map, paste that scene's prompt. Then, in the GM view's **Battle map**, choose **Background image…**, pick the picture and set the hex size.

## Trails of Blood & Dust: Dead Man's Wages

**Series prompt**

```
I'm running a tabletop RPG adventure and need a series of battle maps, one image per scene. Make them one at a time, in the order below, and keep the same art style across the whole series so they look like a set.

Every map follows these rules: Top-down battle map for a tabletop roleplaying game, seen from directly overhead (orthographic, no perspective tilt, no horizon). Fill the whole frame edge to edge: no border, frame, vignette, compass or legend. No grid, no text, no labels, no numbers, no people, creatures, figures or tokens. Floors and ground read clearly as open, walkable space; walls, furniture, rocks and other cover are easy to tell apart from above, with soft shadows that show their height. Landscape, 16:9.

Art style for this adventure: Hand-painted Gothic Western illustration. Palette: dust ochre, sun-bleached timber, bone white and oxblood, with lamp-amber light at night. Gritty texture, like a hand-colored engraving.

The scenes (each with the area it should show):
1. The ride in: something on the trail (about 150 by 85 yards): A wagon trail through dry scrub hills in late-afternoon light, long shadows. A dry wash crosses the trail; boulders, mesquite and a broken fence line give cover. Long drag marks in the dust leave the trail and run toward a cluster of rocks, ending at a dark crevice. A single miner's boot lies beside the marks. On the far edge, the trail drops toward a distant valley.
2. Mercy Springs at noon: the showdown (about 120 by 70 yards): The main street of a quiet silver boomtown at high noon, harsh light and short shadows. Along the street: the Silver Dollar saloon with a covered porch, the sheriff's office, a general store, a boarding house, and a livery stable with a corral. A small wooden church stands at one end with its graveyard behind it. Water troughs, hitching rails, a parked wagon and stacked crates for cover. A dirt mine road leaves town at the far end toward the hills. Doors and windows barred.
3. Mercy Springs by night: the night watch (about 120 by 70 yards): The same town street at night: every window shuttered and dark except the saloon's, moonlight on the dust, a few lanterns. Behind the livery stable, a fresh sinkhole has opened in the ground, with a rough tunnel mouth going down into the dark. (If your image tool can edit, start from the noon map and change it, so the layout matches.)
4. The graveyard: the empty grave (about 40 by 22 yards): A small frontier graveyard behind a wooden church at night, lit by one lantern and moonlight. Wooden crosses and a few stone markers in uneven rows, a sagging picket fence, a dead tree. One fresh grave is dug out from below, its coffin lid split and scratched; three older graves beside it have collapsed inward, opened from beneath and empty. The church's back door and steps at one edge.
5. The Lazarus mine: the burial cave (about 50 by 28 yards): Underground, almost dark: pools of light from a dropped oil lamp and a lantern on a timber. On one side, the lowest drift of a silver mine: timber supports, ore-cart rails, a cart on its side, a crate of dynamite. A broken wall opens from the drift into a much older natural cave lined with burial niches cut into the rock, old bones and grave goods in them, scattered miners' gear on the floor. At the back of the cave, a heap of stones covers something large. Narrow places and pillars of rock for cover.
6. Midnight at the Crane house (about 45 by 25 yards): A wealthy mine owner's house at midnight, the roof removed so the ground floor shows from above: a front porch, entry hall and staircase, a parlor with a piano and fireplace, a study with a desk and an iron safe, a kitchen and back door. Outside: a gravel drive with a carriage, a picket fence, a garden. Moonlight outside; one lamp lit in the study.

Start with scene 1. After each one, wait for me to say "next" before making the next. When a scene says it's the same place as an earlier one, keep that earlier map's layout exactly and change only what the scene describes.
```
