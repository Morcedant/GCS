Fracture Lines

Your ship is old, your debts are real, and the job on the board is the only way to keep flying.

The inhabited regions of the Orion Spur and the Sagittarius Arm are home to civilizations separated by thousands of years of technological development, radically different biological origins, and competing ideas about government, freedom, commerce and survival. Ancient empires are collapsing, but their armies remain dangerous. Commercial powers grow more influential every year. Entire worlds wait behind collapsed corridors to be rediscovered.

Start playing
  1. Open the creator. It walks you through five steps and checks every rule as you go. Or load one of the ready-made characters.
  2. Choose a role and species. Your role's core skills are marked for you; your species sets your attributes and Edges.
  3. Save your character file. Bring it to the table, print the sheet, or keep it in the browser.
  4. Read the Players' Guide. It's in the creator's guide tab: the full rules, the setting and its dangers.
  5. Playing online? Join your GM's shared table from your character's Play tab, or roll with the Discord dice bot while the GM shares their screen.
