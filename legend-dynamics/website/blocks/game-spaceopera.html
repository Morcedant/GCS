Hard Light

The Concord holds a hundred worlds together with treaties, trade and a navy stretched too thin. Beyond its borders lie uncharted systems, ancient ruins that still hum with power, and the Hegemony, an empire that wants everything the Concord has. Hard Light is a space opera setting for the Legend Dynamics RPG system: a starship crew of many species, exploring, trading, fighting and talking their way across the Reach.

Start playing
  1. Open the creator. It walks you through five steps and checks every rule as you go. Or load one of the ready-made characters.
  2. Choose a role and species. Your role's core skills are marked for you; your species sets your attributes and Edges.
  3. Save your character file. Bring it to the table, print the sheet, or keep it in the browser.
  4. Read the Players' Guide. It's in the creator's guide tab: the full rules, the setting and its dangers.
