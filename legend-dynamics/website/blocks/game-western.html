Trails of Blood & Dust

Something followed the wagons west. Most folk never see it. You do, and you hunt it.

The railroad is coming, and it is bringing more than settlers. In the fog-bound timber towns and lamplit mining camps, the old things of the world have followed the wagons west: wolves that walk as men, the dead that will not stay buried, pale strangers who only come out after dark, and witches who know their names. Trails of Blood & Dust is a Gothic Western for the Legend Dynamics RPG system. Most people never see the darkness. You do, and you stand between it and them.

What makes it different
  • Monsters with a weaknessRumor isn't truth. Investigate the thing, name it, and earn the lore card that says how it dies.
  • Hunters of every stripeA marshal, a doctor, a preacher, a gambler who reads the cards, a werewolf in chains, a dead man who got back up.
  • ShowdownsNoon on Main Street: a staredown of Will, then the draw, and a shot nobody can dodge.
  • The trail and the lawDays of open country between towns, and a Heat track that puts your face on a poster when the hunt goes public.

Start playing
  1. Open the creator. It walks you through five steps and checks every rule as you go. Or load one of the ready-made characters.
  2. Choose a role and origin. Your role's core skills are marked for you; your origin sets your attributes and Edges.
  3. Save your character file. Bring it to the table, print the sheet, or keep it in the browser.
  4. Read the Players' Guide. It's in the creator's guide tab: the full rules, the setting and its dangers.
  5. Playing online? Join your GM's shared table from your character's Ride tab, or roll with the Discord dice bot while the GM shares their screen.
