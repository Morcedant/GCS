From first look to first roll

Three steps: pick a world, make a character, and play. You don't need to read the rules first: the character creator explains each choice as you make it, and the rules are there when you want them.

  1. Choose a setting. Four games share one set of core rules: Trails of Blood & Dust, gothic western; Fracture Lines, science fiction; The Shattered Crown, sword and sorcery; Aurora, superheroes. Each page shows who you can play and the setting's guides.

The game in two minutes

How it works

Everything else in the rules builds on these six ideas. The character creator does the arithmetic for you.

  • Roll lowEvery skill has a number, usually 10 to 15. Roll three six-sided dice: a total equal to or under your number succeeds. The further under, the better it goes. A 3 or 4 always works; a 17 or 18 always fails.
  • Easier or harderThe GM adds +2 when things are in your favor, or −2, −4 when they aren't. A friend who helps first can add +1. You only roll when failing would matter.
  • Close isn't failureMiss by 1 or 2 and the GM may offer success at a cost: you get what you wanted, but something else goes wrong. You choose.
  • FightsOn your turn, move and do one thing. If an attack hits, the target can try to dodge (once a round). A hit marks boxes by weapon: 1 for fists, 2 for a sword or a gun, 3 for the deadliest. Three boxes and you're down; a heavy hit leaves you Dying until a friend patches you up.
  • Legend pointsEach setting names them (Grit, Fortune, Resolve, Spark). You start each session with two: spend one to reroll, to take a box less from a hit, or to have packed just the right thing. Earn more when your character's flaws get them into trouble.
  • Consequences and secretsTracks fill as your deeds catch up with you: the law noticing, debts, the dark learning your name. When something strange turns up, investigating it earns a Legend card: what you found out, kept for good.
An example

Your Firearms is 13. Taking a shot across a street, you roll 4 + 3 + 5 = 12: a hit, by 1. The outlaw tries to dodge: his Dodge is 10 and he rolls 11, so the bullet finds him and your pistol marks 2 boxes. Had you rolled 14 and missed, you could have spent a Legend point to roll again.

Who needs what
  • A player needsA character from the creator, and three six-sided dice or the roller on its sheet. To play online: the code or link for the GM's shared table, or an invitation to the group's Discord server.
  • The GM needsThe setting's creator, open on its GM view: everyone's characters, the battle map, the encounter panel and the clocks. The setting's guides, the GM's Guide included, in the creator's Guide tab or from its page. To play online: start a shared table there, or add the dice bot to your Discord server and share your screen.
