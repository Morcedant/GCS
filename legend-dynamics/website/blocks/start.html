From first look to first roll

Three steps: pick a world, make a character, and play. You don't need to read the rules first: the character creator explains each choice as you make it, and the rules are there when you want them.

  1. Choose a setting. Four games share one set of core rules: Trails of Blood & Dust, gothic western; Fracture Lines, science fiction; The Shattered Crown, sword and sorcery; Aurora, superheroes. Each page shows who you can play and the setting's guides.
    Trails of Blood & DustFracture LinesThe Shattered CrownAurora
  2. Create a character. Open the setting's character creator. It walks you through five steps and checks every rule as you go, or you can load a ready-made character. Save the file, print the sheet, or keep it in your browser.
    Trails of Blood & Dust creatorFracture Lines creatorThe Shattered Crown creatorAurora creator
  3. Join or run a game. Round a table, bring your sheet and three six-sided dice (or use the sheet's roller). Online, play on the shared table built into every creator, or over Discord with the GM sharing their screen and the dice bot in the channel.
    Use the shared tablePlay over Discord
Restricted playtest

The shared table is in a restricted playtest. For now, only GMs with an access code can start a table on it; players can join any table their GM starts, with its code. Want to run a game on it? Ask for a GM access code.

Who needs what
  • A player needsA character from the creator, and three six-sided dice or the roller on its sheet. To play online: the code or link for the GM's shared table, or an invitation to the group's Discord server.
  • The GM needsThe setting's creator, open on its GM view: everyone's characters, the battle map, the encounter panel and the clocks. The setting's guides, from its page. To play online: start a shared table there, or add the dice bot to your Discord server and share your screen.
