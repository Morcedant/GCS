Aurora: mind powers, a Mind track and Mind Defense

28 October 2026 · Setting rules change

  • Every character has a Mind track of three boxes beside the injury track: Shaken (−1 to mental actions), Reeling (−2), Broken (unconscious or catatonic). Psychic attacks mark it; it never leaves anyone Dying, and a night's sleep clears it. It's on the play tab, the printed sheet, the GM view's cards and the battle map.
  • Mind Defense from Telepathy, Mind Control, Possession or an ExLAW psi-dampening headset: a weaker mind power does nothing, an equal one is resisted at +2. A Class IV mind against someone without it is resisted at −2.
  • Psychic attacks: Telepathy II (a psychic stab) and Illusion III (fears made real), one Class below the power, Powers against Will, never Dodge, Toughness or Force Field.
  • Rules for reading minds (and being noticed), mind links, being controlled (the player keeps playing; +2 against a harmful command, +4 against hurting a friend; Spark for another roll), emotions, illusions and sensing lies.
  • The Encounter panel resolves psychic attacks as Will contests on the Mind track (Lord Marrow's nightmare no longer meets Toughness or Dodge), and the sheet's roller applies the Mind penalty to mental actions.

Aurora: speed, flight, height and falling

28 October 2026 · Setting rules change

  • Speed, Flight and Super Leaping move a range band in a fight: Medium at Class I, Long at II, Extreme at III, anywhere in the scene from IV. The sheet's Move shows it.
  • Speed's steps, evened out: acts first (I), a second defense and blows at speed (II), two actions once a scene (III), two actions a round (IV), three (V). Blows at speed hit one Class below your Speed. Celerity's now hit at Class III.
  • Chases against vehicles: a car is Class I, a helicopter II, a jet III, a fighter IV; a higher Class wins without a roll.
  • Height counts in the distance (and on the battle map, where a token can have a height). Dives hit one Class higher; carrying slows you a Class; a flier stunned, knocked out or knocked back falls unless they make a Powers roll.
  • A fall is an attack with a Class by its height (I to IV), so Toughness protects against it.
  • The Encounter panel puts Speed first in the turn order, gives the extra actions and the second defense, offers a Dive to fliers, and warns when a flier falls.

Aurora: super strength, and every power's attacks in the Encounter panel

28 October 2026 · Setting rules change

  • Super strength has its own rules: lifting (one step past your Class is a push), objects with a Class to break, throwing (Athletics; a car also hits everyone next to the target), weapons in strong hands (the higher Class; ordinary weapons break above Class II), knockback from Class III, and pins that hold against weaker targets (Body −2 per Class of difference).
  • Strength and Size give +2 to Body for feats and breaking holds: a new choice in the sheet's roller.
  • A player character's attack powers now come into the Encounter panel at their Class: Strength (blows and throws), Natural Weapons, Blast, Telekinesis, Elemental and Weather Control, Gravity, Shadow, Manifest and Stretching. Before, a hero with powers fought there with fists and gear only.
  • The Encounter panel applies strong hands and logs knockback and how hard a pin is to break.

Aurora: Exposure, villains' files and team-ups

28 October 2026 · Setting rules change

  • A character with a Secret Identity has Exposure, a track of four boxes: marked when the secret slips, cleared by alibis. When it fills, the hero is unmasked and the player chooses the price. It shows on the play tab and in the GM view.
  • Exposure is relative: each hero keeps a list of who already knows the secret (family, a team, ExLAW, a villain). A legal status adds its own (Registered: the EMD; Reserve Agent: ExLAW's Director). Exposure measures the secret getting past them, and someone new who learns it goes on the list.
  • Villains keep files on the heroes: what they know, and +2 against that hero when it applies. The GM records them in the GM view; a hero who finds a file sees it on their sheet, and can destroy it.
  • Team-ups: two heroes combine powers in one action. The attack counts one Class higher and both mark a box of Strain. The Encounter panel has a Team-up choice for attacks with a Class.

Aurora: stats for every named character

26 October 2026 · Setting rules change

  • Every Notable Operant in Aurora's GM's Guide now has a stat block: Class, attacks, Dodge, boxes, Defense, powers, skills and what makes them who they are at the table. Autuarch, master of the White Hand, gets an entry of his own.

The campaign builder, with card triggers

25 October 2026 · Website and wording

  • The GM view has a Campaign tab: the campaign's premise and themes, an outline of adventures, people, places, factions, and sessions (the plan, the log and a recap for the players). Save it to a file, or open one.
  • Roll any of them from the setting's own tables: adventure seeds, key characters, places, threats, allegiances and names. A rolled entry opens in its form, to change before you keep it.
  • Triggers: a step of the outline can stay locked until the group holds the cards it names, and as many as you ask for. Then it unlocks for good and tells the GM what happens.
  • Share any entry with the players: they see its public part on their play tab, through the shared table; never a secret or the GM's notes. A shared locked step shows as locked, and what opens it if the GM says so.

Homebrew: your own threats, weapons, Edges, cards, powers, spells and rituals

23 October 2026 · Website and wording

  • The GM view has a Homebrew tab: make your own threats, weapons and Edges with simple forms, no code. They work like the setting's own, marked homebrew, in the Encounter panel, on the battle map, in characters' gear and the roller, and in the creator's Edges.
  • Cards, spells and rituals too: homebrew lore cards, data records, dossiers and tales join the subjects the GM records findings with (and Trails' Hunter's Almanac); homebrew hexes, cantrips and psionic powers, and Aurora's powers (what each does at Class I to VI), join the creator's choices; homebrew rituals make a ritual book beside Rituals by heart.
  • Players get the GM's homebrew when they join the shared table, or from a homebrew file.
  • Bonuses the table works out, chosen from menus: a weapon's accuracy, strong hits and what it's made of; a threat's hard hide, weaknesses, what alone can hurt it, how hard it is to hit and a second defense; an Edge's skill, Dodge or Move bonus, a second defense or shrugging off a telling hit; an attack spell, cast in the Encounter panel. The sheet, the roller, the shared table and the Encounter panel use them.
  • The core rules' Making your own chapter gives the numbers to keep homebrew in line with the book's.

Fracture Lines: example data records, the Countdown and skill

22 October 2026 · Setting rules change

  • Seven example data records in the GM's Guide: a Halvane lattice core, the Warden of the Ashkarr, a corridor engine, a spatial fracture, a living hull, corporate black-site encryption and an alien weapon cache, each with what goes wrong in each phase.
  • The Countdown no longer runs on routine discovery: the GM starts one after a failed roll or a success at a cost, when the stakes are real.

Trails of Blood & Dust: special loads and the hunter's shot

22 October 2026 · Setting rules change

  • Special loads: silver rounds, rock salt shells, cold iron shot, blessed rounds and incendiary shells, tracked as three uses a job each rather than counted bullets. Silver bullets in the gear list become silver rounds.
  • A load works against the weakness it's made of, whoever fires it. With a lore card for the creature and the matching load, a hunter's shot makes the called shot or effect the card permits.
  • Coming by loads can cost: buying silver marks Whispers only if folks learn what it's for, and church silver or the makings of fire mark Heat. Blessed rounds burn a demon but never banish it.
  • A wraith can now be hurt by rock salt in the Encounter panel, as its weakness already said.

Legend cards and the price of information

21 October 2026 · Rules change

  • A Legend card records one thing the characters have established: the finding, its limits, the evidence or source, how it was learned, what it cost, and who holds it. Each setting names its cards.
  • A card can be earned by a discovery clock, or directly: seen or tested firsthand, a reliable account, or the subject's own word backed by evidence.
  • Information is never free: each direct source has a price, ticking the threat clock or a track. A clock's card costs what the clock already cost. Proposing an idea is free.
  • A real weakness works whether or not anyone holds a card for it.
  • A new section for the GM: making Legend cards.

Trails of Blood & Dust: renewing a talisman

19 October 2026 · Setting rules change

  • A broken talisman (the Talisman Edge) is renewed only between jobs, and only by going back to its maker or another worker of the same tradition. Until then it stays spent.

Movement, reach and range bands

18 October 2026 · Rules change

  • Tighter range bands: Short 25 yards, Medium 100, Long 300, Extreme 1,000; Close stays 10. Running covers 20 yards.
  • Move: each character has a Move in yards, worked out from their attributes, Athletics and origin; Run is twice Move.
  • Weapon reach: every weapon in hand has a reach of 1, 2 or 3 yards.
  • Injury and hard going slow Move; stepping away from a fight is free.
  • Telling-hit effects depend on the weapon: Pin needs a weapon in hand, fists or a grapple.
  • Origin speed adjustments, and mounts and vehicles.

Balance revision

17 October 2026 · Rules change

  • Fine weapons and tools: +2 to one narrowly defined use of a skill, never a defense.
  • Additional Defense: a second defense each round at −2.
  • Magic limits: Quicker hand, every hostile burst names a defense or a resistance, personal magic marks at most 3 boxes, and charms still face the target's defense.
  • Telling hits: Break gear, Pin, Precise and Knock down clarified, with firm limits.
  • Medical treatment: one field-treatment and one proper-care attempt per set of injuries.
