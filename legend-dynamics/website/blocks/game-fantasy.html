The Shattered Crown

A thousand years ago the Crown of Aeon bound a sorcerer-empire together. When the last emperor died, the Crown shattered into nine shards, and the empire with it. Each shard gives power to whoever holds it, and each slowly devours them. Now warlords, sorcerer-princes and merchant-kings fight over the empire's corpse, and the old things beneath its cities are waking.

Start playing
  1. Open the creator. It walks you through five steps and checks every rule as you go. Or load one of the ready-made characters.
  2. Choose a role and ancestry. Your role's core skills are marked for you; your ancestry sets your attributes and Edges.
  3. Save your character file. Bring it to the table, print the sheet, or keep it in the browser.
  4. Read the Players' Guide. It's in the creator's guide tab: the full rules, the setting and its dangers.
  5. Playing online? Join your GM's shared table from your character's Adventure tab, or roll with the Discord dice bot while the GM shares their screen.
