{{origin}}, Edges and attributes

All five attributes start at 10, then your {{originLower}} adjusts them. Your {{originLower}}'s tier sets how many Edges you get (3 points split between them). Spend 5 attribute points on top, plus any from Edges. The starting maximum is 13, plus any {{originLower}} bonus to that attribute. You can also build your own {{originLower}} with the GM: choose Custom {{originLower}}… at the end of the list (see Making your own).
