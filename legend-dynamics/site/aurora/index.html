Aurora

In April 1938 the sky burned green for four weeks. The auroras killed five million people and left behind the first Operants: men and women with powers no science could explain. For decades the world pretended they were myths. Then, in 1952, a battle at Fort Knox made pretending impossible, and the law has been trying to catch up ever since, through the Extraordinary Law Enforcement Agency (ExLAW).

Start playing
  1. Open the creator. It walks you through five steps and checks every rule as you go. Or load one of the ready-made characters.
  2. Choose a role and origin. Your role's core skills are marked for you; your origin sets your attributes and Edges.
  3. Save your character file. Bring it to the table, print the sheet, or keep it in the browser.
  4. Read the Players' Guide. It's in the creator's guide tab: the full rules, the setting and its dangers.
  5. Playing online? Join your GM's shared table from your character's Play tab, or roll with the Discord dice bot while the GM shares their screen.
