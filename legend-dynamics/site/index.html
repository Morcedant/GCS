Legend Dynamics RPG system

Heroes are made of skill, nerve and a little luck.

A tabletop roleplaying system built on three six-sided dice and concrete skills. One core, many worlds: Gothic Westerns, starships, swords against sorcery, and superheroes under the law.

Start here Choose a game Read the core rules Play online Why I made it

Legend Dynamics RPG is a living game system. The rules and online tools are available now and continue to evolve through playtesting and player feedback. Tell me how your game went.

  • Roll 3d6 under your skillOne roll for everything. Your sheet shows the number to beat, and the margin tells you how well you did.
  • Skills, not classesTrain what your character knows. A specialty is worth +3, an expert's craft +5; everything else is plausible, just harder.
  • Flaws that payComplications cause trouble at the worst moment, and earn the points that let you reroll, soak a wound or be ready.
The games
Gothic Western

Trails of Blood & Dust

The railroad is coming, and it is bringing more than settlers. In the fog-bound timber towns and lamplit mining camps, the old things of the world have followed the wagons west: wolves that walk as men, the dead that will not stay buried, pale strangers who only come out after dark, and witches who know their names. Trails of Blood & Dust is a Gothic Western for the Legend Dynamics RPG system. Most people never see the darkness. You do, and you stand between it and them.

Science fiction

Fracture Lines

The inhabited regions of the Orion Spur and the Sagittarius Arm are home to civilizations separated by thousands of years of technological development, radically different biological origins, and competing ideas about government, freedom, commerce and survival. Ancient empires are collapsing, but their armies remain dangerous. Commercial powers grow more influential every year. Entire worlds wait behind collapsed corridors to be rediscovered.

Sword and sorcery

The Shattered Crown

A thousand years ago the Crown of Aeon bound a sorcerer-empire together. When the last emperor died, the Crown shattered into nine shards, and the empire with it. Each shard gives power to whoever holds it, and each slowly devours them. Now warlords, sorcerer-princes and merchant-kings fight over the empire's corpse, and the old things beneath its cities are waking.

Superheroes

Aurora

In April 1938 the sky burned green for four weeks. The auroras killed five million people and left behind the first Operants: men and women with powers no science could explain. For decades the world pretended they were myths. Then, in 1952, a battle at Fort Knox made pretending impossible, and the law has been trying to catch up ever since, through the Extraordinary Law Enforcement Agency (ExLAW).

Play online

Create characters, share dice rolls, and play together on a shared map.

  • The shared tableBuilt into every character creator. The GM starts a table and sends the players a code; each joins from their character sheet. Everyone sees the same dice, characters and battle map on their own phone, tablet or computer. Nothing to install.
  • Screen share and the Discord dice botOr the GM shares their screen in a Discord call (or any video call), and players roll with the Legend Dynamics dice bot in the channel.

The odds

Roll three dice and total them; at or under your target is a success. A natural 3 or 4 always succeeds and a 17 or 18 always fails. Most trained skills sit at 12 to 14.
