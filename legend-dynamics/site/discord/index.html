Play on Discord

One dice bot runs every Legend Dynamics setting. It rolls 3d6 under your target and keeps your table's points, clocks and tracks. The commands are the same everywhere; only a few names change with the setting.

Getting started
  1. The GM picks the setting. Run /game set once per Discord server; /game show checks it.
  2. Type / in the game channel. Discord lists every command and shows each option as you fill it in.
  3. Bring your sheet. The bot doesn't know your character: read your target number off your sheet and add up every modifier yourself.

For players

The examples use Dust & Damnation names; swap in your own skill, foe and track. “Legend points” are your setting's points: Grit, Resolve or Fortune.

CommandWhat it does
/roll target:13 modifier:-2 label:ShootingRoll 3d6 under your target. Use the number from your sheet, and add up every modifier yourself: circumstance, injury, assistance. The bot shows the result, the margin and your chance. Spend a Legend point to reroll lets you reroll once, and the second result stands.
/contest target:13 opponent:14 opponent_name:"Bandit" label:GrappleAn opposed roll. The bot rolls both sides and names the winner. Ties and double failures go to the defender, which is the opponent unless you add defender:me.
/assist target:12 helper_target:14 helper:@name label:"Hold the door"Someone helps you. They roll first (Athletics, say): success gives +1, success by 5 or more +2, and a natural 18 brings a complication. Then you roll with their bonus.
/points showYour Legend points this session. Add player:@name to see someone else's.
/points spendSpend a Legend point without rolling, e.g. to reduce an injury by one box or to establish a small preparation.
/points gainGain a Legend point, e.g. when you accept a complication the GM offers. With Extra Legend, use it once at the start of a session.
/track showThe shared setting's tracks (listed under Differences by setting) and what each one means right now.

Only the player who rolled can press the reroll button. Rolls that miss by 1 or 2 remind the GM that success at a cost is on offer.

Clocks

A clock is a circle cut into 2, 4, 6 or 8 segments: 2 for something quick, 4 for urgent or simple, 6 for substantial, 8 for long or complex. A danger clock fills when things go wrong (a failed roll, a success at a cost, time passing), and when it fills the danger happens. A progress clock fills as the characters succeed: one segment for a success, two for success by 5 or more. A chase is a progress clock for the pursuers racing a danger clock for the quarry's escape.

CommandWhat it does
/clock showEvery clock running on this server, with how full each one is.
/clock new name:"The mob forms" segments:4 kind:dangerStart a clock. Use kind:progress for one that fills on successes.
/clock tick name:"The mob forms"Fill one segment. Add segments:2 for two (success by 5 on a progress clock), or segments:-1 to undo a tick.
/clock remove name:"The mob forms"Delete a clock once it has filled or no longer matters.
/seedThe adventure seed's last line is a ticking clock, with the /clock new command to start it.

Anyone can start, tick or remove a clock, so players can tick a progress clock after a success; usually the GM runs them. Ticking or removing a clock needs its full name, though capitals don't matter. A server can run up to 20 clocks at once.

For the GM

GM commands need the Manage Server permission on the Discord server. Everyone can see clocks, tracks and points.

CommandWhat it does
/game set game:Dust & DamnationChoose this server's game. Do it once; /game show checks it.
/points newsessionStart a session: everyone goes back to two Legend points, and unused points are gone.
/points gain player:@nameAward a Legend point, e.g. for an accepted complication.
/points set player:@name points:3Correct a player's total.
/track mark name:heatMark a box on a track; a partial name works ("heat" finds Heat). /track clear clears one, and /track reset clears them all.
/seedRoll an adventure seed: one line from each seed table, and the command to start its ticking clock.
/contest ... defender:meWhen a player resists you (a grapple, a lie, a chase), they are the defender: ties and double failures go to them.

Each server keeps its own points, clocks and tracks, and tracks are kept separately per setting, so switching settings doesn't lose them.

Everything not listed here works the same in every setting, clocks included: the commands, sizes and rules don't change, only the dangers they count down to. A partial track name works: heat finds Heat, dark finds The Dark.
