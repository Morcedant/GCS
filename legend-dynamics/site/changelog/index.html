Homebrew: your own threats, weapons, Edges, cards, spells and rituals

23 October 2026 · Website and wording

  • The GM view has a Homebrew tab: make your own threats, weapons and Edges with simple forms, no code. They work like the setting's own, marked homebrew, in the Encounter panel, on the battle map, in characters' gear and the roller, and in the creator's Edges.
  • Cards, spells and rituals too: homebrew lore cards, data records, dossiers and tales join the subjects the GM records findings with (and Trails' Hunter's Almanac); homebrew hexes, cantrips and psionic powers join the creator's choices; homebrew rituals make a ritual book beside Rituals by heart.
  • Players get the GM's homebrew when they join the shared table, or from a homebrew file.
  • The core rules' Making your own chapter gives the numbers to keep homebrew in line with the book's.

Fracture Lines: example data records, the Countdown and skill

22 October 2026 · Setting rules change

  • Seven example data records in the GM's Guide: a Halvane lattice core, the Warden of the Ashkarr, a corridor engine, a spatial fracture, a living hull, corporate black-site encryption and an alien weapon cache, each with what goes wrong in each phase.
  • The Countdown no longer runs on routine discovery: the GM starts one after a failed roll or a success at a cost, when the stakes are real.
  • A character trained in the skill that fits the subject can skip Confront the unknown.
  • Rival clocks: an ordinary danger clock for whoever is racing the crew to the same find.

Trails of Blood & Dust: special loads and the hunter's shot

22 October 2026 · Setting rules change

  • Special loads: silver rounds, rock salt shells, cold iron shot, blessed rounds and incendiary shells, tracked as three uses a job each rather than counted bullets. Silver bullets in the gear list become silver rounds.
  • A load works against the weakness it's made of, whoever fires it. With a lore card for the creature and the matching load, a hunter's shot makes the called shot or effect the card permits.
  • Coming by loads can cost: buying silver marks Whispers only if folks learn what it's for, and church silver or the makings of fire mark Heat. Blessed rounds burn a demon but never banish it.
  • A wraith can now be hurt by rock salt in the Encounter panel, as its weakness already said.

Legend cards and the price of information

21 October 2026 · Rules change

  • A Legend card records one thing the characters have established: the finding, its limits, the evidence or source, how it was learned, what it cost, and who holds it. Each setting names its cards.
  • A card can be earned by a discovery clock, or directly: seen or tested firsthand, a reliable account, or the subject's own word backed by evidence.
  • Information is never free: each direct source has a price, ticking the threat clock or a track. A clock's card costs what the clock already cost. Proposing an idea is free.
  • A real weakness works whether or not anyone holds a card for it.
  • A new section for the GM: making Legend cards.

Fracture Lines, Aurora and The Shattered Crown: data records, dossiers and tales

21 October 2026 · Setting rules change

  • Fracture Lines keeps data records in an archive, Aurora dossiers in a binder, and The Shattered Crown tales in a saga, each with its own chapter in both guides.
  • The Shattered Crown gains discovery clocks, racing its Doom track.
  • New advantages give three starting cards the player writes: secrets from their role or origin that aren't common knowledge.
  • Trails of Blood & Dust: each source of a lore card now has its price.

Trails of Blood & Dust: renewing a talisman

19 October 2026 · Setting rules change

  • A broken talisman (the Talisman Edge) is renewed only between jobs, and only by going back to its maker or another worker of the same tradition. Until then it stays spent.

Movement, reach and range bands

18 October 2026 · Rules change

  • Tighter range bands: Short 25 yards, Medium 100, Long 300, Extreme 1,000; Close stays 10. Running covers 20 yards.
  • Move: each character has a Move in yards, worked out from their attributes, Athletics and origin; Run is twice Move.
  • Weapon reach: every weapon in hand has a reach of 1, 2 or 3 yards.
  • Injury and hard going slow Move; stepping away from a fight is free.
  • Telling-hit effects depend on the weapon: Pin needs a weapon in hand, fists or a grapple.
  • Origin speed adjustments, and mounts and vehicles.

Balance revision

17 October 2026 · Rules change

  • Fine weapons and tools: +2 to one narrowly defined use of a skill, never a defense.
  • Additional Defense: a second defense each round at −2.
  • Magic limits: Quicker hand, every hostile burst names a defense or a resistance, personal magic marks at most 3 boxes, and charms still face the target's defense.
  • Telling hits: Break gear, Pin, Precise and Knock down clarified, with firm limits.
  • Medical treatment: one field-treatment and one proper-care attempt per set of injuries.

Changes before October 2026 aren't listed here.
