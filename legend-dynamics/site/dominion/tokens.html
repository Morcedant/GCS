Fracture Lines: Dominion · ship tokens

Every people builds its own ships, with its own outlines and class names (what each ship does is the same for everyone). Each empire's section has three tokens of each ship in its colour at 0.9 inch, one plain to colour in, and two at 0.7 inch. Then the ships that belong to no one, and markers for what sits in orbit. Print at 100% (no scaling), cut along the circles, and point the arrow on the rim the way the ship is heading.

Peoples Hex tokens to mark whose a world is: set one on the world's hex. Portraits and traits are on the Peoples page.

The Concord Human · Restless energy: Colony and outpost ships cost 25% less, and new colonies start with 2 population.

The Tessan Dynasties Tessan · Old money: +25% credits from every world, and trade treaties pay double.

The Kel Hives Kel · Hive-cities: Worlds hold 2 more population, and population grows faster.

The Hegemony Remnant Ozhvek and Dresh · Imperial legions: Warships hit on +1, and troop transports land twice the force.

The Vael Living Fleets Vael · Living hulls: Ships move 1 hex farther and repair a point of hull every turn, anywhere.

The Mirrun Commonwealth Mirrun · Pelagic scholars: +20% science on every world, and Ocean worlds hold 3 more population.

The Ylith Aeries Ylith · Far sight: Ships and worlds see 2 hexes farther, and scouts and colony ships move 1 faster.

The Zhaq Free Republics Zhaq · Never again: Their worlds' garrisons are half again as strong and never weaken under siege, and a besieged world starves half as fast.

Markers Set one under a world's token, or (a station) on an empty hex for a deep-space station.

Orbital station A forward base in orbit, at a colony or an outpost (or, built by an outpost ship, in deep space): light guns (4 hull, one shot of 2, or +4 hull to a battery's), your ships here repair 2 hull a turn, it sees 2 hexes farther, and it gives supply like a fuel depot.

Orbital battery 6 hull, 2 shots of 2 a round, repairs 2 a turn.

Fuel depot Processes the system's volatiles into fuel: the outpost gives supply, and fleets refuel here.

The Free Traders At peace with everyone but the pirates.

Merchant convoy hull 2, unarmed · speed 3. Free traders, carrying goods between the Exchanges. They pay both ends when they arrive, if the pirates don't get them first.

Pirates At war with every power.

Raider hull 2, skill 11, 1 dmg · speed 3. A pirate gunship: fast, lightly armed, and after your credits.

Pirate haven hull 8, skill 12, 2 dmg ×2 · never moves. A fortified pirate base in an empty system. It sends out raiders until someone burns it.

The creatures of the deep At war with every power.

Crystal swarm hull 5, skill 11, 1 dmg ×3 · never moves. Living crystal that nests on rich worlds and shreds anything that comes near. It never leaves.

Void leviathan hull 12, skill 12, 3 dmg · speed 1. A creature the size of a moon, drifting between the stars. It fights whatever it finds.

Warden husk hull 10, skill 12, 2 dmg ×2 · never moves. A dead Warden machine from an older war, half-awake in the Graveyard. It fires on any warship that comes near, and its wreck is full of old science.

The Wardens The late-game crisis: the Signs, then the waking.

Watcher hull 6, skill 12, 2 dmg · never moves. A Warden machine half-buried by a rift, dormant but watching. It fights whatever comes near. When the Wardens wake, it wakes as a Node.

Warden Node hull 10, skill 12, 2 dmg ×2 · never moves. A Warden forge, built on a world they've burned. It sends out hunters and holds the system. While one stands, a new Heart can grow.

Sentinel hull 3, skill 12, 1 dmg · speed 3. A Warden hunter: quick, and never alone.

Colossus hull 6, skill 13, 2 dmg · speed 2. A Warden war machine the size of a moon's mountain.

Heart hull 20, skill 14, 3 dmg · never moves. The Wardens' mind and forge. It never moves, mends itself, and sends out the waves. Break it and they go dark.
