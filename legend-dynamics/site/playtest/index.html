Play it, then tell us how it went

Thanks for playtesting Legend Dynamics. You don't need to read anything first: the GM brings your character and explains as you go. This page has the two-minute version of the rules, and, after the game, a few questions that shape the next version.

Go to the questions
Before you play

The game in two minutes

  • Roll lowYour character sheet gives each skill a number, usually 10 to 15. Roll three six-sided dice: a total equal to or under the number succeeds, and the further under, the better. A 3 or 4 always works; a 17 or 18 always fails.
  • Say what you doDescribe what your character tries. The GM tells you which skill to roll, and gives +2 when things are on your side or −2 when they aren't. You only roll when failing would matter.
  • Close isn't failureMiss by 1 or 2 and the GM may offer success at a cost: you get what you wanted, and something else goes wrong. You choose.
  • FightsOn your turn, move and do one thing. When something hits you, you can try to dodge, once a round. Each hit marks boxes on your injury track; three boxes and you're down.
  • Legend pointsYou start with two. Each game names them: Grit in Trails of Blood & Dust, Resolve in Fracture Lines, Fortune in The Shattered Crown, Spark in Aurora. Spend one to reroll, to take a box less from a hit, or to have packed just the right thing. You earn more when your character's flaws get them into trouble.
  • Find out what it isWhen something strange turns up, dig into it. What you prove becomes a card on your sheet (a lore card, a data record, a tale or a dossier): what the thing is, and how it's beaten.
For the GM

Battle maps

A map for each scene of the four first adventures. To use one, download it, then in the GM view open the Battle map, choose Background image…, pick the picture, and set the hex size shown on its card, so distances and range bands come out right. At a table, you can also print it or put it on a screen.
