This book is for whoever runs the game. It gathers what the GM reaches for at the table: case seeds, the notable Operants of the world, the threats with their numbers, and how to use the GM view's battle map and Encounter panel. The rules themselves are in the **Players' Guide**; this book builds on them.

# Running cases

# Notable Operants

Ages are as of 2025. Each comes with a stat block, and each is in the GM view's Encounter panel as a threat, under their name and Class. They use the rules for powers: an attack's Class against their Defense decides what gets through.

**Mr. Marvel (Hector Gonzalez), 113.** At 31, Hector Gonzalez was Nikola Tesla's friend and lab assistant. Given Ambrosia in 1943, he gained an alternate, idealized form, and chose to stay on Earth while Tesla and Kessler journeyed through Otherworld. Mr. Marvel flies, has enormous strength, fires electrical bolts and is incredibly tough, and doesn't age; Gonzalez does, slowly, thanks to the Ambrosia. He is a family doctor in Los Angeles, still able to call up Marvel when needed, and worried by the impatience and anger that come with the form.

*Class IV.* **Attacks** Brawling 15 (Strength IV); Powers 15 (electrical bolt, Blast IV). **Dodge** 11. **Boxes** 6. **Defense** IV (Toughness). **Powers** Strength IV, Toughness IV, Flight IV, Blast IV, all in the Marvel form; Transformation (the form, one action to call up). **Skills** Medicine 15, Leadership 14, Persuasion 13, Read People 13. **Two lives.** As Hector Gonzalez he has no powers and Medicine 15. In the Marvel form his impatience and anger rise: a Will roll to walk away from a fight he's winning.

**Celerity (Ava Torres), 33.** The super-fast Latina heroine of New York, who protects the innocent and defends the helpless. She fought the Umkas in 2012, and Steel Hawk nearly killed her; the Golden Centurion got her help, but she spent a year in a coma. Since waking she has dedicated herself to helping people across the country. She befriended the Green Man and persuaded him to open the Haven, and has stood between federal authorities and the refugees at every confrontation. There is a warrant for her arrest; ExLAW's Operants aren't pursuing it, yet.

*Class IV.* **Attacks** Brawling 14 (blows at speed, Class III). **Dodge** 14 (a second defense each round). **Boxes** 4. **Defense** I (friction-proof skin). **Powers** Speed IV (two actions every round), Toughness I, Adaptation I (friction and wind). **Skills** Athletics 15, Persuasion 13, Streetwise 13, Read People 13. **Faster than the fight.** Two actions a round, and a second defense. A federal warrant hangs over her: helping her marks Scrutiny.

**The Green Man (Micky Holt).** A struggling folk singer, exposed to something... different on a peyote-driven walkabout in the Southwest. It drove him insane, though he still loves to play. His power over plants raised an impenetrable forest across southern Indiana in a matter of days: from Bloomington to the Ohio River you travel on foot, on horseback or by boat. He offers sanctuary to anyone who swears to protect the Earth and spread its message, and serves only vegetarian meals. Any attempt to burn or harm the forest ends in horror-movie destruction by rampaging plants. The Haven, his refuge for America's homeless, is the one place he allows fire, for warmth and cooking.

*Class V.* **Attacks** Powers 15 (roots and thorns, Class IV in the Mad Forest, III outside it). **Dodge** 9. **Boxes** 5. **Defense** II (a mantle of bark). **Powers** Elemental Control (plants) V inside the Mad Forest, III outside it; Regeneration II; Animal Control II. **Skills** Persuasion 13, Read People 12, Medicine 12, Occult 12 (and a fine singing voice). **The forest is his.** In the Mad Forest two wildwoods answer him each round he asks. Anyone who burns or harms the forest is the forest's enemy. Mad: what he wants changes with the song.

**Michael Lance, 47.** A 1940s private investigator pulled into a timeless Limbo during a case in 1946, and released by the breach in 2012. He wields a mystic amulet inherited from his grandfather, bonded to him by the spirit of **Em Antu**, the Egyptian priest and sorcerer who made it. It lights a room, fires a laser that cuts steel, and casts realistic illusions. He has used it against all manner of strange beings in New York's shadows, and against the duplicates of Shadow Earth.

*Class III.* **Attacks** Firearms 13 (a .38 revolver, Class I); Powers 14 (the amulet's beam, Blast III). **Dodge** 11. **Boxes** 4. **Defense** none. **Powers** through Em Antu's amulet: Blast III (a light that cuts steel), Illusion III, Mystic Sight II, light at will. **Skills** Investigation 15, Streetwise 14, Firearms 13, Occult 12. **The amulet.** Without the amulet he's a 1940s private eye with a revolver. Em Antu's spirit has its own opinions, and sometimes its own plans.

**Steel Hawk, about 110.** An Olympic athlete and German attorney in Brussels, kidnapped in 1952 while prosecuting a wealthy industrialist and taken to a Foundation black lab. There he was tortured and exposed to an artifact that turned his body to living metal, charged it with a spectral energy, let him regenerate from terrible wounds and stopped him aging. He escaped with no memory of who he had been, and with an anger and a need to control everything around him. He became a mercenary for the Morcain; when he discovered the Foundation's part in his making, his revenge put him in the crosshairs of governments worldwide. In 2012 he helped Autuarch open the breach, came to regret it, and helped close it.

*Class IV.* **Attacks** Brawling 15 (Strength III); Powers 15 (spectral energy, Blast IV). **Dodge** 10. **Boxes** 5. **Defense** IV (living metal). **Powers** Toughness IV, Strength III, Blast IV, Regeneration III, Flight II, Immortality (ageless only). **Skills** Law 14, Athletics 14, Leadership 13, Firearms 13. **Control.** He must run every room he's in. Orders make him dig in; a deal that leaves him in charge can buy him for a while.

**Chat Roux (Red Cat).** An officer of a multi-dimensional law enforcement organization, stranded on this Earth when his transport was destroyed during the Umkas invasion. He now works for the French **Affaires spéciales**, undercover behind a holographic mask from an Earth far more advanced than ours. He worries the mask will fail without proper maintenance.

*Class II.* **Attacks** Firearms 14 (dimensional sidearm, Class II); Brawling 13 (unarmed, Class I). **Dodge** 12. **Boxes** 4. **Defense** I (uniform armor). **Powers** no powers of his own; an advanced holographic mask (Illusion II: any face), a dimensional sidearm (Class II) and armor (Defense I). **Skills** Investigation 14, Stealth 14, Law 13, Engineering 12. **The mask.** On a natural 17 or 18 with Deception, the mask flickers. Without repairs from his own Earth, one day it will fail for good.

**Vanguard ("Louis Feslen").** Able to manifest any firearm he can imagine. Believed to be a French expatriate with military and martial-arts training; wanted by the Affaires spéciales, Interpol, MI5 and ExLAW for at least seventeen deaths worldwide. Rumor says every one of them was a Shadow Earth minion. No one has confirmed it.

*Class III.* **Attacks** Firearms 16 (a manifested firearm, Class III); Brawling 14 (martial arts, Class I). **Dodge** 12. **Boxes** 4. **Defense** I (manifested body armor). **Powers** Manifest III (any firearm he can imagine, and its ammunition). **Skills** Firearms 16, Brawling 14, Stealth 14, Athletics 13. **Seventeen kills.** Wanted on four continents. If the rumor is true, every target was a Shadow Earth duplicate, and he knows how to spot them.

**Abd al-Rahman.** An Iranian Operant criminal who opposes the People's Republic. Remarkably strong, tough and brilliant, he leads a band of mercenaries for the highest bidder. He may be able to teleport; no official source will comment.

*Class IV.* **Attacks** Brawling 15 (Strength IV); Firearms 13 (rifle, Class I). **Dodge** 10. **Boxes** 5. **Defense** III (Toughness). **Powers** Strength IV, Toughness III, Super Intellect II, Teleport II (rumored, and true). **Skills** Leadership 15, Science 14, Firearms 13, Persuasion 13. **Mercenary leader.** Never alone: four Morcain enforcers' worth of mercenaries. Once a fight, he Teleports out with one passenger.

**The Golden Centurion, about 2,050.** An immortal warrior born in ancient Rome, cursed (the current story goes) by Jupiter to wander the Earth unaging for his crimes against the gods, running off with a Vestal Virgin among them. He regenerates quickly, is incredibly tough and cannot die; but he feels everything, even when his head is cut off. Brutal and unyielding in his moral code, he gives himself entirely to whichever cause he chooses.

*Class V.* **Attacks** Melee 16 (gladius and strength, Class IV). **Dodge** 11. **Boxes** 6. **Defense** IV (Toughness). **Powers** Toughness IV, Strength IV, Regeneration V, Immortality V (Mythic). **Skills** Melee 16, Athletics 15, Leadership 13, Occult 12. **Cannot die.** He clears a box every turn and comes back within an hour from anything, but he feels every wound. His code is iron: he never breaks his word, or forgives a broken one.

**The Shadow Knight (Grant Douglas).** The North American Knight of the *Ordo Viarum Lucidarum*: a former Marine and mercenary who somehow came into possession of several supernatural artifacts. He leads a small team of specialists in a private war against monsters and Operants who threaten human lives.

*Class III.* **Attacks** Melee 15 (consecrated blade, Class III); Firearms 14 (sidearm, Class I). **Dodge** 11. **Boxes** 5. **Defense** II (warded mail). **Powers** through his artifacts: a consecrated blade (Class III, one Class higher against spirits and monsters), Warding III, Mystic Sight II, warded mail (Defense II). **Skills** Occult 14, Melee 15, Leadership 13, Firearms 14. **The Order.** He brings his specialists: two or three ExLAW-grade operatives. Lose the artifacts and he's a very good ex-Marine.

**Lord Marrow (Altari Marrow).** A self-proclaimed magus with illusory and telepathic powers, who brings his victims' fears to life. He can't control minds, but he can terrify people into handing over money, jewels or secrets his weak mental scans can't find. Strong minds resist him, so he researches his victims to find the perfect fear. He lives somewhere in Europe and works for the Morcain on special occasions; his reliance on horror-film images betrays a lack of imagination.

*Class III.* **Attacks** Powers 15 (a living nightmare, Class II; Will resists). **Dodge** 9. **Boxes** 3. **Defense** none. **Powers** Illusion III (fears made real), Telepathy II (shallow scans). **Skills** Deception 15, Investigation 13, Persuasion 13, Read People 13. **Strong minds.** A character with Will 13 or more resists his illusions at +2. He studies his victims first: if he knows your fear, the nightmare is Class III.

**The Ice Reaper (Hyun Wang).** A South Korean gangster who found an artifact that bonded an *aleum sasin*, an Ice Reaper, to him. He can become the bloodthirsty spirit at will, and used it to become the *Sangsanim*, boss of his country's underworld. He sits on the 12.

*Class IV.* **Attacks** Melee 16 (the Reaper's scythe of ice, Class IV); Powers 14 (killing cold, Blast III). **Dodge** 11. **Boxes** 5. **Defense** III (the Reaper's form). **Powers** Transformation (into the aleum sasin, at will): Natural Weapons IV, Toughness III, Elemental Control (ice) III, Phasing II. **Skills** Leadership 14, Streetwise 15, Persuasion 14 (threats), Melee 16. **Bloodthirsty spirit.** In the Reaper's form he must take a life before the scene ends, or mark Strain. One of the 12: harming him draws the Morcain.

**Helios (David Carlysle Jones), 44.** A former Army major recruited into DIRE and given a heavily modified Ambrosia. The transformation was agonizing, and made him one of the most powerful Operants alive: he absorbs and redirects sunlight as energy blasts or flight, and is nearly invulnerable and remarkably strong. His weakness: eight hours without sunlight (the moon and stars don't count) leave him near human.

*Class V.* **Attacks** Powers 16 (solar blast, Class V); Brawling 14 (Strength III). **Dodge** 11. **Boxes** 6. **Defense** IV (Toughness). **Powers** Energy Absorption (sunlight) V, Blast V, Flight IV, Toughness IV, Strength III. **Skills** Firearms 14, Leadership 14, Piloting 13, Athletics 13. **Sunlight.** Eight hours without sunlight (not moonlight or starlight) and every power drops to Class I until he's back in the sun.

**Zephyr (Dr. Alemayehu Okafor).** A famed Ethiopian archaeologist who pursues cryptozoological artifacts with militant zeal. A native Operant with heightened strength and resilience and the power to control the air, he is an avowed environmentalist, wanted in several countries for alleged attacks on industrial and mining concerns in Africa.

*Class III.* **Attacks** Powers 15 (a hammer of wind, Class III); Brawling 13 (Strength II). **Dodge** 12. **Boxes** 4. **Defense** II (Toughness). **Powers** Elemental Control (air) III, Flight II, Strength II, Toughness II. **Skills** Science 15 (archaeology), Occult 13, Athletics 13, Persuasion 12. **Eco-saboteur.** Wanted in several countries. He spares workers and strikes machines; give him a polluter and he may become an ally.

**Evelyn Dark, 25.** Born in Avalon to a line of witches descended from Hecate and Freya, with a rare gift for manipulating energy and matter. A seer foretold she would bring Avalon's doom, and at thirteen she fled to Earth in defiance of the Accords. She gathered homeless children into a ring of thieves and con artists, the **Dark Ones**, until the Foundation caught her two years later. Its experiments heightened and destabilized her powers; one destroyed the lab and the town around it, and she was the only survivor. In the decade since, she has used her powers sparingly, helping where she can and hiding from Earth's most powerful magic users.

*Class V.* **Attacks** Powers 16 (raw energy, Class V). **Dodge** 12. **Boxes** 4. **Defense** III (a force field). **Powers** Cantrips IV, Blast V, Telekinesis IV, Force Field III, all of it heightened and destabilized by the Foundation. **Skills** Deception 14, Stealth 14, Streetwise 13, Occult 14. **Unstable.** On a natural 17 or 18 with Powers, the power hits everyone nearby. Using a power above Class III draws Avalon's hunters (Scrutiny).

**Milon Dubinsky, about 650.** Once a king in what is now Romania, who killed every man in a conquered village so he could marry the chieftain's wife. She poisoned him before the wedding, and when he begged for a cure, cursed him to undying life as a wolfman, unable to return to human form. He found he could share part of his curse with the descendants of his tribe (supernaturally tough and able to change back, but mortal), and over six centuries has built a small army of werewolves to do his bidding.

*Class IV.* **Attacks** Brawling 17 (claws and fangs, Class IV). **Dodge** 12. **Boxes** 6. **Defense** III (the curse). **Powers** Natural Weapons IV, Strength IV, Toughness III, Regeneration IV, Immortality IV (Mythic), Enhanced Senses III. **Skills** Brawling 17, Leadership 14, Stealth 13, Athletics 14. **The wolf-king.** Silver ignores his Defense and stops his Regeneration. He never fights without three Dubinsky werewolves, and can't take human form.

**Nikola Tesla, 169.** Rarely on our Earth now. He prefers his pocket dimension, 1884 Ivry-sur-Seine, where his laboratory has doors to many other dimensions, Shadow Earth among them. He experiments with inventions to enhance or modify Operant abilities, including the metatropic manipulations called magic. He has devised a way to trap Richard Kennedy, and hasn't used it: he doesn't want to interrupt the man's good works.

*Class VI.* **Attacks** Powers 15 (a lightning projector, Class IV). **Dodge** 9. **Boxes** 3. **Defense** IV (a personal field). **Powers** Super Intellect VI, Gadgeteering VI (doors between worlds), and whatever devices he brings: a personal field (Force Field IV), a lightning projector (Blast IV). **Skills** Science 18, Engineering 18, Occult 15, Persuasion 13. **Not really here.** He arrives and leaves through a door from 1884 Ivry-sur-Seine. He has a way to trap the Atomic Man, and won't use it.

**The Atomic Man (Dr. Richard Kennedy), about 100.** A nuclear scientist and cousin of the political dynasty, irradiated in 1959 when he pushed his colleagues to safety from a runaway reactor. As he lay dying, an interdimensional entity offered to bond with him so he could continue his work and protect his world. He agreed, and was healed: he controls the strong and weak nuclear forces around him, and his mind holds the entity's intellect as well as his own. Strength, speed, invulnerability, energy blasts, flight. He doesn't seem to age, though in 2024 he found a grey hair. He supports democratic causes worldwide, protects civilians in war, and has destroyed whole arsenals to free nations from oppression. The US, China and Russia once built a weapon together to kill him; it failed. Only Sergei Grachyov has stood against him, in the 2007 battle that destroyed the Washington Monument. Grachyov's rumored cancers are blamed on Kennedy.

*Class VI.* **Attacks** Powers 17 (nuclear force, Class VI); Brawling 16 (Strength VI). **Dodge** 13 (a second defense each round). **Boxes** 8. **Defense** VI (Toughness). **Powers** Strength VI, Toughness VI, Blast VI, Flight VI, Speed IV, Super Intellect V (the entity's mind beside his own). **Skills** Science 18, Leadership 15, Persuasion 14, Read People 14. **Protector.** He won't risk a civilian, ever: put one in the line of fire and he stops. **Sergei Grachyov.** Considered one of the strongest men on Earth, he serves the Foundation as troubleshooter and chief recovery expert for the empowered, traumatized and psychopathic escapees from its black labs. Wanted worldwide, he is considered a weapon of mass destruction by himself. He has been reported dead several times; it never sticks. He has fought the Golden Centurion and Vanguard to a standstill.

*Class V.* **Attacks** Brawling 17 (Strength V). **Dodge** 10. **Boxes** 7. **Defense** V (Toughness). **Powers** Strength V, Toughness V, Regeneration III. **Skills** Brawling 17, Athletics 15, Investigation 13 (recoveries), Streetwise 13. **It never sticks.** Reported dead several times. # Dossiers

Running investigations and recording what the team learns. The rules players use are in the *Dossiers* chapter of the Players' Guide; here is the GM view's Discovery panel, for clocks and dossiers.

## Making dossiers

For the GM: when a dossier is earned, and how to write one. A dossier is a record of what the characters established, so writing it well matters more than how often you hand one out.

1. **Decide that something is established.** A dossier is earned when a discovery clock fills, or when the characters see or test something themselves, hear it from a source you judge reliable, or are told it by the subject itself with evidence to back it up. A guess, however right, isn't established, and neither is a rumor. Ask what supports their idea; when the answer is good, let them act on it, and give the dossier once they've confirmed it.
2. **Write the finding narrowly.** One fact, in the words the characters would use, and no more than they found out: "bright light drove it back", not "it fears light". Two separate facts make two dossiers. Never write the subject's whole truth onto a dossier; later discoveries add more dossiers.
3. **Write its limits.** What the finding doesn't show: how often it was seen, under what conditions, and what is still unknown. The limits are what keep the next investigation interesting.
4. **Note the evidence or source.** What they saw, tested, read or were told, and by whom. A source's reputation doesn't make it right: if it was only probably true, the limits say so.
5. **Charge the price.** A dossier from a discovery clock costs nothing more: the scenes, failed rolls and pushes were its price. Any other source has its price (see *Earning a dossier*): choose the one that fits how they learned it, and mark it.
6. **Choose the owner and the recipients.** Whoever did the finding owns the dossier. Everyone who was there, or heard it from a reliable source, gets a shared copy.
7. **Record it.** In the GM view's **Discovery** panel: **Mint** a full clock's dossier, or use **Record a finding without a clock**, which marks the price for you. Then save everyone's characters to file, so the players' sheets carry it. A player can also write a dossier on their own sheet, when you agree it's been earned.

*Example:* **Lord Marrow.** Established: *His bone-shaping stops when he's soaked through*. Limits: *Seen in one fight in the rain; not how long it lasts once he's dry*. Source: The team's own fight with him at the Ironside docks (seen in action). Cost: Collateral +1 (the docks took the damage).

- **When a later finding disagrees.** Write a new dossier that says what they now know; the old one stays on the sheet as what they believed then, and the new one's limits can say so.
- **A dossier is never proof of identity.** Holding a dossier about one subject doesn't prove that the thing in front of them is that subject: the Identify phase does.
- **Weaknesses work anyway.** Don't hold back a real weakness because no one holds the dossier: if they try it, it works.
- **Sealed Binder.** Agree each starting dossier with its player before play: it must come from the character's role or origin, be narrow, and be a real secret: something that isn't in any database, registry or published record. Make sure it fits what you know to be true, adjust it if it doesn't, and keep a note of it.

## The Discovery panel

The GM view's **Discovery** panel runs discovery clocks and the Escalation they race. Start a clock with its segments per phase (or a plain progress clock for a repair or a task in the same race), and fill it as the investigation rolls succeed. It stops at the Identify gate until you name the subject; then it shows every dossier the characters in the GM view already hold for it. The **Failed roll** and **Push** buttons tick the track for how they were digging, and a failed roll ticks the Escalation too. When a clock fills, **Mint** gives the primary investigator the dossier and everyone you tick as present a shared copy. **Record a finding without a clock** gives a dossier for something established directly (seen or tested, a reliable account, a creature's word with evidence behind it) to whoever you tick, and marks its price: the track or the Escalation that source costs. Save all to file afterwards, so the players' character files carry it. The Discord bot's `/discovery` and `/cards` commands do the same at a table that plays online.

# The GM's tools

## The battle map

The GM view of the character creator has a **battle map** for fights where it helps to see who is where. It is a tool, not a rule: everything on it follows the *Personal combat* rules in the Players' Guide, and a fight without it works the same way. Open **How to use the map** on the map itself for the buttons, step by step.

**Two kinds of map.**

- **A hex map** for fights where position matters: cover, doorways, who can reach whom. The GM chooses what one hex is: 1 yard for a brawl in a room, 5 or 10 yards for a street, 50 for open ground. Dragging a token or measuring shows the distance and its **range band**, so the GM can read the penalty from the Range table in the Players' Guide.
- **A range track** for fights where only distance matters: a chase, a running fight across open country. It has a lane for the team and one for each range band. Moving a token into the next lane moves it one band in or out.

**Tokens.** The GM places the team with one button. Their tokens stay linked to the characters in the GM view: marking Injury, Dying, the stunned condition or a Spark point on a token marks it on the character, and the other way round. Opponents come from the setting's threats with their boxes filled in. Their pips fill as they are hurt, and a token whose boxes are all marked is taken out.

**Keeping a map.** The map stays in the GM's browser. **Save map to file** keeps both maps, their pictures and tokens in one file, to prepare a fight ahead of time or share it with another GM.

## The Encounter panel

The GM view also has an **Encounter** panel that runs a fight by the *Personal combat* rules and shows every roll. Add characters from the GM view and opponents from the Threats table (or make one up on the spot), then start: it keeps the turn order by Agility, and for each attack works out the range, the defense (Dodge, Parry or Block, one a round, and a second at −2 with Additional Defense), the boxes marked and any telling hit, offering a Spark point choices at the moment they apply. Hits land on the character cards and the battle map, and with gritty combat on, grievous wounds are rolled and kept. The panel keeps a log and a summary of the fight, which can be saved to a file. It's a helper, not a rule: the GM can rule any result differently, and the log says so.

**The shared table.** The GM can start a shared table from the GM view and give the players its code; each player joins from their character's Play tab. While they're connected, every roll from a sheet is rolled by the table server, so the whole table sees the same dice as they land, in a live log on the Play tab and on the GM view, along with who rolled, against what, and any telling hit. A point reroll answers its own roll, once. Each player's character is shared with the GM too: it appears among the GM view's characters, marked at the table, and its injuries, points, ammunition and form stay in step both ways, whether they're changed on the sheet, on the GM's card or in an encounter. In a fight, each player's Play tab shows the GM's battle map and the encounter's turn order and latest results; a player drags their own token, and on their turn sends the GM what they'll do, which the GM puts into the Encounter panel and runs. The GM can remove a player from the table, and give the table a new code: everyone at it moves with it, and the old code stops working. Without a table, the roller works exactly as it always has.
