The Shattered Crown

Discord commands

The table's Discord bot rolls the dice and keeps the shared Fortune points, clocks and Troubles tracks. The bot doesn't know your sheet, so you give it the target number.

Getting started

  1. Type / in the game channel to see every command.
  2. Pick one; Discord shows each option as you fill it in.
  3. Read your target number off your sheet and add up every modifier yourself.

For players

  • /roll target:14 modifier:-2 label:Melee

    Roll 3d6 under your target. Use the number from your sheet, and add up every modifier yourself: circumstance, injury, assistance. The bot shows the result, the margin and your chance. Spend a Fortune point to reroll lets you reroll once, and the second result stands.

  • /contest target:14 opponent:13 opponent_name:"Serpent-priest" label:Grapple

    An opposed roll. The bot rolls both sides and names the winner. Ties and double failures go to the defender, which is the opponent unless you add defender:me.

  • /assist target:12 helper_target:14 helper:@name label:"Force the tomb door"

    Someone helps you. They roll first (Athletics, say): success gives +1, success by 5 or more +2, and a natural 18 brings a complication. Then you roll with their bonus.

  • /points show

    Your Fortune points this session. Add player:@name to see someone else's.

  • /points spend

    Spend a Fortune point without rolling, e.g. to reduce an injury by one box or to establish a small preparation.

  • /points gain

    Gain a Fortune point, e.g. when you accept a complication the GM offers. With Extra Fortune, use it once at the start of a session.

  • /clock show

    Every clock the GM is running.

  • /track show

    The shared Troubles tracks (Infamy, Doom, Debt, Hunger) and what each one means right now.

Only the player who rolled can press the reroll button. Rolls that miss by 1 or 2 remind the GM that success at a cost is on offer.

For the GM

GM commands need the Manage Server permission on the Discord server. Everyone can see clocks, tracks and points.

  • /game set game:The Shattered Crown

    Choose this server's game. Do it once; /game show checks it.

  • /points newsession

    Start a session: everyone goes back to two Fortune points, and unused points are gone.

  • /points gain player:@name

    Award a Fortune point, e.g. for an accepted complication.

  • /points set player:@name points:3

    Correct a player's total.

  • /clock new name:"The eclipse" segments:6 kind:danger

    Start a clock of 2, 4, 6 or 8 segments. Danger clocks fill on failures and time; use kind:progress for one that fills on successes.

  • /clock tick name:"The eclipse"

    Fill one segment. Add segments:2 for two, or segments:-1 to undo. /clock remove deletes a clock.

  • /track mark name:doom

    Mark a box on a track; a partial name works ("doom" finds Doom). /track clear clears one, and /track reset clears them all.

  • /seed

    Roll an adventure seed: one line from each seed table, and the command to start its ticking clock.

  • /contest ... defender:me

    When a player resists you (a grapple, a lie, a chase), they are the defender: ties and double failures go to them.

Each server keeps its own points, clocks and tracks. If a command is missing from Discord's list, open the bot's /register page once, then press Ctrl+R in Discord.

The Shattered Crown. Commands work the same in every game the bot runs; the examples use this game's names.
