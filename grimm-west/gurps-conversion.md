# Converting the GURPS templates: draft for review

This is the plan for turning the 13 GURPS Character Sheet templates into Grimm Wild West heritages, with two samples (Blutbad and Grimm) converted in full. Once the pattern is approved, the other eleven follow it, and the creator, guide and ready-made posse are updated to match.

Decisions already made:

- **All heritages are playable.** Man-eating Wesen are playable only when the whole table agrees to a horror-tinged game (see section 6).
- **Power tiers are allowed,** with Edges that keep lower tiers equal at the table (section 2).
- **Magic is a light ritual system:** the Lens decides *how* magic is worked, not what it can do (section 7).

## 1. How a template is sorted

A GURPS template is a point budget of many traits. This system uses a few larger pieces, so each template is sorted into layers instead of converted point for point.

| GURPS part | Becomes |
|---|---|
| Human-form attribute increases | **Heritage adjustments**, keeping the template's shape and still totaling zero |
| Traits every Wesen template shares | **Core Wesen rules**, written once (section 3) |
| The full-woge alternate form | A **woge profile** for each kind: what changes while you're woged (section 4) |
| Distinctive advantages | **Heritage advantages**: a narrow +2 or a GM-limited ability |
| That heritage's own disadvantages | **Companion complications** for those advantages |
| Optional Hunted and Patron | A new optional **Allegiance** step for everyone (section 5) |
| GURPS skills | **Suggested Trained skills** for the heritage (Guns → Shooting, Hidden Lore → Wesen Lore or Occult) |
| Point totals | **Power tiers** (section 2) |

## 2. Power tiers and Edges

Heritages differ in power. To keep a Human or a Fuchsbau as useful at the table as a Hexenbiest, every character gets **3 points** to split between heritage power and **Edges**. A stronger heritage costs more of those points and leaves fewer Edges.

| Tier | Heritage power | Edges |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Human | 3 |
| 1 (Minor) | A small woge profile | 2 |
| 2 (Major) | A strong woge profile or a set of gifts | 1 |
| 3 (Apex) | A strong woge profile and a signature power | 0 |

**Edges.** Each Edge buys one of:

- **+1 attribute point** (up to two Edges), within the normal starting maximum.
- **One extra Trained skill.**
- **A second general advantage**, with no extra complication.
- **True grit:** start every session with 3 Grit instead of 2 (once).
- **A friend in town:** a named contact who helps once per case (once).
- **A fine weapon or tool:** an heirloom Grimm weapon, a master's instruments, or a charm-maker's kit. It gives +2 to one skill when it's used (once).

Edges are useful in every scene, not just fights. Heritage power shines mostly when a Wesen is woged, which is also when it costs Exposure. That's the balancing lever.

**Tier 3 costs Exposure.** A full woge by a Tier 3 heritage in front of townsfolk marks **2** boxes of Exposure instead of 1.

## 3. Core Wesen rules (shared by every template)

| GURPS trait (in every Wesen template) | Rule |
|---|---|
| Invisibility (only to humans; switchable) | **Hidden face.** Humans see your Wesen face only when you choose a full woge. |
| See Invisible (Grimm and woged Wesen) | **Wesen sight.** You see other Wesen when they woge, and you see a Grimm's eyes. |
| Alternate Form (Reduced Time 3; Trigger: anger, frustration, fright) | **Woge** as a free action. Anger, frustration or fright can force one. |
| Stress Atavism | **Losing control.** When an enemy appears, you're frightened, or you're attacked, the GM can call for a Will roll. On a failure you woge. |
| Secret (Wesen) (Possible Death) | **The Code.** Exposure is deadly; a full woge seen by townsfolk marks Exposure. |
| Appearance (Monstrous), Social Stigma (Monster) | **Monstrous while woged.** Ordinary people panic or attack, and Persuasion with them is impossible. |
| Frightens Animals | **Animals bolt.** −2 to Riding while woged, and horses won't carry a woged rider. |

## 4. Woge profiles

The woge's huge GURPS boosts can't map one for one onto a 3d6 game, so each kind gets a short profile, using these rules:

| GURPS woge trait | Woge profile |
|---|---|
| ST and HT | **Body bonus:** +1 for each 3 levels of the higher of the two, up to +2. Woge bonuses can exceed the starting maximum. |
| DX | **Agility bonus:** +1 for each 3 levels (rounded), up to +2. |
| IQ | **Intellect bonus**, same scale. |
| Perception, Acute senses, Discriminatory Smell or Hearing | **Keen senses:** tracking by scent or hearing becomes possible, in the same way gear makes a task possible. It's not a +2. |
| Infravision, Night Vision | **Dark sight:** no penalty for darkness. |
| Sharp Claws, Sharp Teeth, Fangs | **Natural weapons:** claws mark 2 boxes, a bite alone marks 1. |
| Damage Resistance 5+, or Injury Tolerance (Damage Reduction) | **Tough:** each hit that marks 2 or more boxes marks one fewer (never below 1). |
| Damage Resistance 2–4 | Nothing on its own; it's folded into the Body bonus. |
| Enhanced Dodge | +1 Dodge while woged. |
| Basic Move, Super Jump, Enhanced Move | **Swift** or **Leaper:** narrative movement, plus +2 to Athletics for that kind of feat. |
| Exotic powers (sonic scream, poison bite, lifedrain, telekinesis, tremor) | **Signature power:** a heritage advantage (ability) or a Tier 3 core feature. |
| Woge disadvantages beyond the core | Companion complications, or **woge drawbacks** that apply while woged. |

## 5. Allegiance (new optional step for everyone)

The Grimm and Incantare templates offer patrons, and several Wesen templates offer optional Hunted. In this system that becomes one optional choice anyone can make in Step 4. It pairs a benefit with a burden, like a heritage advantage.

| Allegiance | Benefit | Burden (earns Grit) |
|---|---|---|
| The Church | Once per case, shelter, records or a favor from the clergy | **Duty:** the Church gives you orders. Sicarii discipline links you to the Order of the Holy Knife. |
| A Royal Family | Once per case, money, papers or a door opened | **Duty:** an involuntary call to serve. Rivals of the family hunt you. |
| A secret society (the Golden Dawn, the Brotherhood of the Holy Earth) | Once per case, lore, a meeting place or a fellow initiate | **Duty and Vow:** keep its secrets and follow its traditions. |
| The Wesen Council | Contacts in the local Wesen community | **Duty:** the Council calls on you. You're its operative, and others know it. |
| A Grimm family | Access to the family books and kin | **Duty:** to the family name, and its enemies. |
| None | — | — |

The optional "Hunted" picks (Church, Royal Family, Secret Society, Wesen Council, or Grimm for Incantare) become ordinary complications: **Hunted by …**

## 7. Incantare: the Lenses as styles

All Lenses can attempt any effect. The Lens decides **how**: time, materials, what it costs, and what it's best at.

**Working a ritual.**

1. Describe the effect. The GM sets its **weight**: minor (2 segments), significant (4) or major (6).
2. Gather energy on a progress clock of that size. Each Occult roll takes the Lens's casting time; success fills one segment, and success by 5 or more fills two. Failures cost time, or tick a danger clock.
3. When the clock is full, the effect happens. A ritual interrupted with energy gathered may go wrong.

**Shared rules** from your house notes:

- **Trappings:** the right materials for the effect (your Decan correspondences) remove one segment from the clock.
- **Charms:** a ritual can be stored in an object and released by breaking it. You can carry as many ready charms as your Occult training allows: Trained 2, Specialty 3, Expert 4.
- **Elixirs and potions** spoil: they stay good until the end of the day, or a week on a success by 5 or more. This replaces "1d6 × Magery hours".
- **Grimms sense magic:** for each charm or potion you carry, a Grimm gets +1 to notice your magic.
- **Higher Purpose** is the Lens's specialty: +2 when the effect fits the Lens's tradition.

| Lens | Style (how magic works for them) | Specialty (+2) | Companion complication (from the template's required disadvantages) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chymiater** (Alchemist) | Brews elixirs, potions and powders ahead of time. Slow to make, instant to use. | Brewing | **Price of the Work:** pick one of the template's vices (Greed, Lecherousness, Alcoholism and so on). Accept it when it bites. |
| **Figmatarum** (Illusionist) | Glamours, illusions and mesmerism, worked through performance, music or art | Illusion and mesmerism | **Artist's Vanity:** the template's artist disadvantages |
| **Magi** (Wizard) | Scholarly ritual from grimoires: broad, but slow and dependent on books | Divination and wards | **Obsession (Magical Knowledge):** required in the template |
| **Malifica** (Witch or Warlock) | Curses and hexes through a link: blood, hair or a treasured object | Curses through a link | **Wicked Reputation:** the template's vices |
| **Phitonissa** (Pagan) | Cunning-folk rites tied to the seasons, nature and the old gods | Nature and folk magic | **Disciplines of Faith (Pagan):** required in the template |
| **Scaenarfex** (Artistic Enchanter) | Enchants objects and works of art; the best charm-maker | Enchanting objects and charms | **Artist's Temperament:** the template's temperament disadvantages |
| **Veneficare** (Sorcerer) | Quick casting (Ritual Adept, Time 3), but only along one or two Paths, with few prepared spells | Its one or two Paths. Its clocks fill two segments per success, but it can hold only one charm. | **Higher Purpose bound:** a single narrow calling it must follow |

## 8. Decisions (settled)

1. **Grimm blood.** As in the template: a Hexenbiest or Zauberbiest who drinks it under a full moon loses their powers, involuntarily, until the *Contaminatio Ritualis* restores them. In addition, a Chymiater can brew a Grimm's fresh blood in a major ritual into a draught that, injected or drunk, neutralizes anyone's magical gifts for 1d6 days.
2. **Fuchsteufelwild.** Playable at Tier 3. Acid Talons became its signature power, Tremor a heritage advantage, and Sadism became the companion complication *Cruel Streak*.
3. **Vallachi.** Horror only, with Naschazer and Taniwha.
4. **Wesen without templates** (Eisbiber, Jägerbär, Bauerschwein, Mauzhertz, Ziegevolk, Steinadler) were adapted from the show. The fandom wiki is blocked from the build environment, so these rest on general knowledge of the series. Confirmed: the Jägerbär Roh-Hatz rite, Ziegevolk pheromones working in human form, Bauerschwein senses working unwoged. Check the rest against your table's canon.
5. **Spellings** follow the show: Blutbad, Hexenbiest, Zauberbiest, Fuchsteufelwild, Jägerbär.

## 9. Where the results live

Every heritage, converted, is in `data/heritages.json`: adjustments, tier, heritage rules, woge profile, suggested skills, heritage advantages with companions, and the source template. `tools/gen-heritages.py` writes that file into the creator and into the guide's **Heritages** chapter, so the two can't drift apart:

```
python3 tools/gen-heritages.py && python3 tools/build-guide.py --game grimm-west --docx
```
