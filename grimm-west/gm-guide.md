This book is for whoever runs the game, and anyone can read it. It gathers what the GM reaches for at the table: case seeds, the threats with their numbers, and how to use the GM view's battle map and Encounter panel. The rules themselves are in the **Players' Guide**; this book builds on them.

# Running cases

# Threats

Opponents use the same rules as the characters. **Minor opponents** (hired guns, a mob, a Royal's footmen) have **one box**: any hit that gets through takes them out of the fight.

| Threat | Attack (target) | Dodge | Boxes | Weapon | Trait |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hired gun (minor) | Shooting 11 | 7 | 1 | Revolver | **Numbers.** Two or three of them; they flee when half are down. |
| Outlaw | Shooting 13, Brawling 11 | 9 | 3 | Revolver, rifle | **Quick draw.** +2 on the first shot of a fight. |
| Blutbad in full woge | Brawling 13, Tracking 14 | 9 | 3 | Claws and fangs (2) | **Relentless.** Ignores the Wounded penalty. |
| Siegbarste | Brawling 14 | 6 | 4 | Fists (2), clubs | **Iron bones.** Bullets and blades mark 1 box at most; a Siegbarste's weakness must be learned. |
| Mauvais Dentes | Brawling 15, Stealth 13 | 10 | 4 | Sabre teeth (2) | **Royal hound.** Hunts one named target and doesn't stop. |
| Hexenbiest | Occult 14, Deception 13 | 8 | 3 | Spells, knives | **Telekinesis.** Once per round, throws a person (1 box) instead of attacking. Grimm blood drunk under a full moon ends her power. |
| Wendigo | Brawling 13, Stealth 14 | 9 | 3 | Claws (2) | **Larder.** Drags victims away alive; a Survival roll finds the cache. |
| Hundjäger (Royal enforcer) | Melee 13, Tracking 14 | 9 | 3 | Blade (2), crossbow | **Pack.** Comes in threes; +1 to attack a target an ally hit this round. |
| Order of the Holy Knife assassin | Melee 14, Stealth 14 | 10 | 3 | Consecrated knife (1) | **Ambush.** +2 on the first attack from hiding; poisons blades (Body roll or 1 more box). |
| Brotherhood druid | Occult 14, Survival 13 | 8 | 3 | Staff, nature rites | **Living ground.** Commands roots, weather or beasts outdoors; powerless on rails and stone. |

**Adjusting difficulty.** For a tougher version, add +1 to its targets and one box; for a weaker one, subtract.

# The GM's tools

## The battle map

The GM view of the character creator has a **battle map** for fights where it helps to see who is where. It is a tool, not a rule: everything on it follows the *Personal combat* rules in the Players' Guide, and a fight without it works the same way. Open **How to use the map** on the map itself for the buttons, step by step.

**Two kinds of map.**

- **A hex map** for fights where position matters: cover, doorways, who can reach whom. The GM chooses what one hex is: 1 yard for a brawl in a saloon, 5 or 10 yards for a street, 50 for open prairie. Dragging a token or measuring shows the distance and its **range band**, so the GM can read the penalty from the Range table in the Players' Guide.
- **A range track** for fights where only distance matters: a chase after a stagecoach, a running fight across open country. It has a lane for the posse and one for each range band. Moving a token into the next lane moves it one band in or out.

**Tokens.** The GM places the posse with one button. Their tokens stay linked to the characters in the GM view: marking Injury, Dying, Dazed or Grit points on a token marks it on the character, and the other way round. Opponents come from the Threats table with their boxes filled in. Their pips fill as they are hurt, and a token whose boxes are all marked is taken out.

**Keeping a map.** The map stays in the GM's browser. **Save map to file** keeps both maps, their pictures and tokens in one file, to prepare a fight ahead of time or share it with another GM.

## The Encounter panel

The GM view also has an **Encounter** panel that runs a fight by the *Personal combat* rules and shows every roll. Add characters from the GM view and opponents from the Threats table (or make one up on the spot), then start: it keeps the turn order by Agility, and for each attack works out the range, the defense (Dodge, Parry or Block, one a round), the boxes marked and any telling hit, offering a Grit point choices at the moment they apply. Hits land on the character cards and the battle map, and with gritty combat on, grievous wounds are rolled and kept. The panel keeps a log and a summary of the fight, which can be saved to a file. It's a helper, not a rule: the GM can rule any result differently, and the log says so.
