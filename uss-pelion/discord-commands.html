USS Pelion

Discord commands

The table's Discord bot rolls the dice and keeps the shared Duty points, clocks and Ship status tracks. The bot doesn't know your sheet, so you give it the target number.

Getting started

  1. Type / in the game channel to see every command.
  2. Pick one; Discord shows each option as you fill it in.
  3. Read your target number off your sheet and add up every modifier yourself.

For players

  • /roll target:16 modifier:-2 label:Sensors

    Roll 3d6 under your target. Use the number from your sheet, and add up every modifier yourself: circumstance, injury, assistance. The bot shows the result, the margin and your chance. Spend a Duty point to reroll lets you reroll once, and the second result stands.

  • /contest target:16 opponent:13 opponent_name:"Klingon warrior" label:Grapple

    An opposed roll. The bot rolls both sides and names the winner. Ties and double failures go to the defender, which is the opponent unless you add defender:me.

  • /assist target:12 helper_target:14 helper:@name label:"Repair the overload"

    Someone helps you. They roll first (Engineering, say): success gives +1, success by 5 or more +2, and a natural 18 brings a complication. Then you roll with their bonus.

  • /points show

    Your Duty points this session. Add player:@name to see someone else's.

  • /points spend

    Spend a Duty point without rolling, e.g. to reduce an injury by one box or to establish a small preparation.

  • /points gain

    Gain a Duty point, e.g. when you accept a complication the GM offers. With Extra Duty, use it once at the start of a session.

  • /clock show

    Every clock the GM is running.

  • /track show

    The shared Ship status tracks (Shields, Hull, Weapons, Engines, Sensors, Communications, Transporters, Life support) and what each one means right now.

The bot's original names still work: /pelion is the same as /roll, and /duty is the same as /points.

Only the player who rolled can press the reroll button. Rolls that miss by 1 or 2 remind the GM that success at a cost is on offer.

For the GM

GM commands need the Manage Server permission on the Discord server. Everyone can see clocks, tracks and points.

  • /game set game:USS Pelion

    Choose this server's game. Do it once; /game show checks it.

  • /points newsession

    Start a session: everyone goes back to two Duty points, and unused points are gone.

  • /points gain player:@name

    Award a Duty point, e.g. for an accepted complication.

  • /points set player:@name points:3

    Correct a player's total.

  • /clock new name:"The time loop resets" segments:4 kind:danger

    Start a clock of 2, 4, 6 or 8 segments. Danger clocks fill on failures and time; use kind:progress for one that fills on successes.

  • /clock tick name:"The time loop resets"

    Fill one segment. Add segments:2 for two, or segments:-1 to undo. /clock remove deletes a clock.

  • /track mark name:shields

    Mark a box on a track; a partial name works ("shields" finds Shields). /track clear clears one, and /track reset clears them all.

  • /seed

    Roll an adventure seed: one line from each seed table, and the command to start its ticking clock.

  • /contest ... defender:me

    When a player resists you (a grapple, a lie, a chase), they are the defender: ties and double failures go to them.

Each server keeps its own points, clocks and tracks. If a command is missing from Discord's list, open the bot's /register page once, then press Ctrl+R in Discord.

USS Pelion. Commands work the same in every game the bot runs; the examples use this game's names.
