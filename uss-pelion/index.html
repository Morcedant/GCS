        Quick reference

        Resolution
        3d6 at or below adjusted skill. Natural 3–4 always succeeds; 17–18 always fails. Margin = target − roll.
        Opposed
        Success beats failure; both succeed → larger margin wins; ties and double failures favor the defender.
        Combat
        Move a short distance and take one action. Hit → target may Dodge once per round → weapon effect. Partial cover −2.
        Phasers
        Stun: target rolls Body −2, fail = out for the scene, success = −2 next action. Lethal: two boxes.
        Medicine
        Stabilize: one action. Field care clears 1 box (2 on margin 5+). Sickbay +2, clears 2 (3 on margin 5+).
        Duty points
        Reroll (keep second), reduce an injury by one box, or establish a small preparation.
